Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Research Deck is a personal automation used only by its owner, Nate Hukill. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

  • Google Drive, Slides and Sheets: only files the app itself creates (the weekly slide deck and a research log spreadsheet). It cannot see any other files.
  • Gmail: send-only permission, used to email the weekly deck to the owner. It cannot read, search or delete any email.

How data is used and stored

Data is used solely to produce the owner's weekly research deck. Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's computer and as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation account. Public research content is sent to the Anthropic API for summarization. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Research Deck's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
