Research Deck

A private, single-user research tool.

Research Deck is a personal automation operated by Nate Hukill. Once a week it gathers publicly available investment research (papers, videos, articles), summarizes it, builds a slide deck in the owner's own Google Drive, and emails that deck to the owner's own Gmail address.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
